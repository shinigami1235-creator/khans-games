# Khan's Games

Three browser games in one file. No install, no internet needed once loaded.

- **Tank** — 1–2 players. Your tank always drives forward. Hold ◀ ▶ to steer, FIRE to shoot. Shells bounce twice and can't hurt you. Grab the `?` crates.
- **Brick Duel** — 1–2 players. Paddles on the walls, a brick field in the middle. First to 7.
- **Dino Rush** — 1 player. Sixteen hazards, coins, and a double jump.

Play: open `index.html`, or visit the hosted link and add it to your home screen.

Built by Khan.
